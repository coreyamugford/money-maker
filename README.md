# Money Maker

A 3D money-making life-sim browser game. Start broke with a cardboard rocket and a camera, film videos, build better rockets, build your town from scratch and become a Ridiculousinaire.

Play it here: https://coreyamugford.github.io/money-maker/

Walk around your house, your basement Rocket Lab and the town in first person. Build homes, shops and a bank, invest your money, try your luck at the casino, and race the other players to the top.

Controls: WASD to walk, move the mouse to look, E or click to use things, Tab opens your phone, V switches the camera, P pauses, M mutes.

Works best in Chrome on a computer. Your game saves in your browser.
